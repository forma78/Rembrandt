Pipes of paint in whole lanes, Master Flo's way: every lane the whole pipe in one run, inside lane first, so a pipe crossing itself steps one lane at a time. Paints across the pipe, a tube edge to edge.

Whole lanes: each lane is one run from the start of the pipe to its end, always the same way, the inside lane first; the brush lifts at the end and flies back. It is not washed between lanes, so it starts the next lane with what it carried out of the last. Where it passes wet paint it loads some of it and carries it on, so where a pipe crosses itself the later lane lies on top, one lane per step. Marks go right across the pipe, one paint each, by hand with the Marks tool or every spacing from the segments' paints. A pipe is a line of points with rounded corners: lines and arcs only. Seams cut it into segments. An Along segment is brushed in lanes that follow the line, each lane with its own paint, so the colours run across the pipe like light on a tube. An Across segment is brushed in short strokes over the pipe, back and forth, so the colours change along it in rings. At a seam the brush stops and starts again. A Fan end is brushed in rays towards the end of the line. Marks are split into dashes, one paint each; the paint map shows which.
