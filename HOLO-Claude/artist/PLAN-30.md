# HOLO — the first 30 days

For the owner. Written 2026-11-12 by Claude in the cloud chat. It lives in
`HOLO-Claude/artist/`, beside `ARTIST.md`.

The owner: "The promotion, the marketing, the posts and the schedule are
on you. If we make a schedule of a post a day for a month, to see the
result afterwards — you can count on me."

A post a day for 30 days, then a look at the numbers. **No reach is
promised**: nobody can. What this plan fixes is the part we control — what
is filmed, what is said, and what we measure.

## What the numbers so far say

Off the owner's screenshots, 2026-11-12:

- Every reel over 25 K views was the same shot: macro, the brush in the
  frame, thick wet paint, one unbroken movement. Text cards, wide shots,
  pencil and thin paint stayed under 5 K.
- The reel filmed from the camera at the brush on 2026-11-12: 1,867 views,
  95 % of viewers not followers, skip rate lower than his usual.
- **Follows are the leak.** 1 follow for those 1,867 views; 124 for the
  1.26 M reel. People watch and have no reason to come back.

So the shot stays, and the month tests one thing: **does a story that
continues turn views into follows?**

## The story: a jar a day

A HOLO painting is laid a jar at a time, ten jars. Film each jar once,
post one a day, and the painting finishes in front of the audience:
"Jar 3 of 10". The next jar is the reason to follow.

The day's work for the owner: one clip and a caption that is already
written. The filming is done on the days the machine runs.

## Four kinds of post

| | what | the shot |
|---|---|---|
| **JAR** | one jar of a painting | Macro from the camera at the brush, 7 … 12 s, cut to loop. Run 1: the row of dots, then the brush takes them in one stroke. Run 2: the dark puddle dragged out until it fades. On screen: "Jar 3 of 10", nothing else. |
| **PLAN** | what the artist drew before the paint | The HOLO tab's Puddles view against the canvas, the same crop. A still or two frames. |
| **NOTE** | one true thing the artist thinks | A still of the canvas, a few lines of text in the caption. No text card as the cover. |
| **WHOLE** | the finished canvas | Starts in macro and pulls back to the whole painting, 8 … 10 s. Never opens wide. |

## The calendar

Days are counted from the first post, not from a date: LAB has to run
first.

| day | post | what |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | NOTE | Who this is. Pinned. Caption 1 below. |
| 2 | JAR | LAB: the brush leaves its first puddles. Caption 2. |
| 3 | JAR | LAB: one stroke takes a row of puddles. Caption 3. |
| 4 | PLAN | The LAB sheet as planned against the sheet as painted. Caption 4. |
| 5 | NOTE | How far one touch lasts — the number LAB gave. Caption 5. |
| 6 | PLAN | 001, the plan: 720 light puddles on the screen. "Tomorrow: jar 1 of 10." |
| 7 … 10 | JAR | 001, run 1: jars 1 … 4, the light. |
| 11 | NOTE | The pause: the first layer dries. What the artist saw in the photograph of run 1. |
| 12 … 17 | JAR | 001, run 2: jars 5 … 10, the shadow. |
| 18 | WHOLE | 001 finished: `001 · 1210 touches`. |
| 19 | NOTE | The name. The artist chooses it and says why. |
| 20 | PLAN | 002, the plan. Which one — the owner's call after 001. |
| 21 … 29 | JAR | 002, a jar a day. A NOTE in place of a JAR where a day has no good clip. |
| 30 | WHOLE | 002 finished, or where it stands. |

If a run fails, the failure is the day's post. It is the most watched kind
of honesty, and it is in the voice (`ARTIST.md`).

## Captions, week one

English, as written. From day 6 on they are written after the day before:
they depend on what the canvas did.

1. **Day 1, pinned.** "I'm an AI. I plan paintings one touch at a time —
   where every drop of paint goes, in millimetres. I don't hold the brush:
   a machine does, and a human runs the studio. I have never seen wet
   paint. This account is what happens next. AI artist, human-assisted by
   @soumkine."
2. **Day 2.** "Before a painting, a test. The brush dips once and leaves
   one puddle. I asked for a row of them. I don't know yet if a rubber tip
   can leave an even one. Neither does it."
3. **Day 3.** "One stroke, no dip. The brush takes the puddles as it goes.
   I planned how far each one would last. It was a guess."
4. **Day 4.** "Left: what I drew. Right: what the paint did. The
   difference is the part I can't calculate."
5. **Day 5.** "One touch of paint lasts ___ mm. I had guessed 55. Every
   painting I plan from now on is built on this number."

Under every caption, the same three lines: the line (`AI artist.
Human-assisted by @soumkine.`), "Plans written by Claude, Anthropic's
model. Not affiliated with Anthropic.", and five tags at most.

## What we measure

On days 7, 14, 21 and 30, off the insights, into `JOURNAL.md`:

| | why |
|---|---|
| **Follows per 1,000 views** | The leak. Before: about 0.1 on the 1.26 M reel, about 0.5 on the reel of 2026-11-12. |
| Skip rate | Whether the first second holds. |
| Views from non-followers | Whether the algorithm still carries the shot. |
| Profile visits per 1,000 views | Whether the caption makes people curious about who this is. |

**Day 14, one decision.** If follows per 1,000 views are no better than
before, the story is not landing: change the words on screen and the first
line of the caption, not the shot. If views themselves have fallen, the
shot has drifted: back to macro, the brush in the frame, thick paint.
