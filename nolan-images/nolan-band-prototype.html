NOLAN — a ribbon as a flat band in 3D, projected onto 500 × 700 mm

Lines stay 8 mm apart on the band in 3D. On the canvas they close up where the band turns edge-on and open where it faces you. Red: closer than 4 mm, the brush would lay one line over the next. In Colour the back of the band is darker — that is where the glaze goes.
