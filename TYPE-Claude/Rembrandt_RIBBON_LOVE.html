REMBRANDTRIBBON

LOVE as one ribbon: L, O, E, V, clockwise, no branches, one run.

Canvas, mm
Shape, in band widths
Brush
Paint
Files for the machine

One ribbon from the top of the L to the tip of the V. The E's middle and lower arms and the V's right arm are folds: the ribbon goes out, turns, and comes back alongside itself. The O's bottom is painted twice, once as the L's foot and once closing the loop; the brush leaves the O where the two meet and hooks down into the E.
