- **A pill**: a segment ≈ 25 mm closed into a ring with a rim and a bead

- **A cap at every turn of the snake**: solid ≈ 13–15 mm, overhanging the
  leg's end by ≈ 5 mm (a ruler in the frame, read off the photo); between

So a jar paints a range, not its colour: its masstone in the heads and the
caps, film and ground between (`OPTICS.md` §1, the knowledge base in the
cloud chat). Drops first would give every leg its paint along it, with no
restart inside a leg.

Against it, HOLO's own measure: on the painted-over white the drag scraped,
a clean channel 3–4 mm, a ridge at each side, a heap 15–23 mm where the
stroke ends (`HOLO-Claude/JOURNAL.md`, 2026-11-14 and 15). The sheet says
whether FORM-G's ground does the same.

## B, the run: drops first

A group at a time: four neighbouring legs of one jar, FORM-G's columns,
5 mm apart.

1. **Four drops, one a leg**, each a dab as HOLO lays one
   (`holoplan.js`, `dabsOn`; a mark of 8 mm, est.), a dip each.
2. **At once the drag**: a snake of four strokes, one a leg along its
   middle, the brush down at its turns as in DRAG SNAKE; no dip in it; the
   J3 compensation as FORM-G's.
3. **No idle run, no legs + 1.** Not HOLO V4's 2.5 mm, nor its 3.75 mm
   idle tracks: 5 mm, the brush's width.
4. Then the next group.

B is a choice of FORM-G's run beside DRAG SNAKE; the default stays as it is.

Open, the owner's call before the first run:
- **Where a leg's drop stands.** A reading, not his word: at the end its
  stroke starts from, so the snake's drops alternate ends and each heap
  falls at the other end. HOLO's lesson of 2026-11-15 was one end starved,
  then dots at both ends.
- **A leg longer than one drop feeds.** HOLO's drag carried its paint to
  the leg's end and heaped it there; Mickey's legs run far past 80 mm
  (est.). The sheet keeps to 80.

- **A**: FORM-G as it runs now: DRAG SNAKE, its idle run, a dip every
