# GOGH — painting in dabs

The owner, 2026-11-22: "painting in strokes, like real painting"; "in GOGH we copy FORM-G's functionality fully … the one
difference: in GOGH there will be no long strokes! All of them drops of 2 cm, as on IMG_3377". The tab: `app/gogh.html`;
its generator GEN-V, `app/genv.html`; the canon: README, "GOGH: a dab, a dip, nothing after it".

## generator/

`genvpic.py`: an approved GEN-V sketch into a card of GOGH's library, `app/gogh/<id>.json` and its `.jpg`, true to the sketch
(as `FORM-G/generator/genfpic.py`); `--local` for a picture of a brand or of another's work, `app/gogh/local.json`.
Pillow in a venv, as FORM-G's generator.
