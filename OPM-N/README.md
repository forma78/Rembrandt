brush under test, Da Vinci no. 2 impasto 7105, flat, up to the fifth colour, a round
brush from the sixth, Blush Pink, on (21:26), Da Vinci no. 1 impasto 7705** (the owner: "it is a pity if we spoil a
canvas during the tests; let our miracle machine train on paper for now"), not
the Raphaël 0 round that Track width and Dip every were measured with. So what these photos
show belongs to this brush on this paper. The first run stopped at 17:44:50 on
one missed read of the shoulder; the second went on from cell 8 by Go to.

its way back from the jar: "shoulder: ok Z 3", the answer to J later than the
0.25 s it is waited for; the arm reached 0 by itself, the brush up.
